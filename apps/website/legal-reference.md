# Legal page design

Inspected [VVD terms](https://vvd.world/terms) and [VVD privacy](https://vvd.world/privacy) on 6 October 2026.

Both use the existing dotted navy surface and a centered 768px document column. At 1280 × 720, the title starts at y=128, uses a 36px/40px bold sans face, and has a 16px gap before its muted 14px/20px revision date. The document starts at y=252: an 18px rounded panel with a 10%-white surface, 20%-white border, 8px backdrop blur and 32px desktop padding. Section labels use 16px/24px medium sans; paragraphs and lists use 14px/20px Space Grotesk, with 24px between sections. Mobile panel padding becomes 24px.

Rawan uses the same measured surface, column, glass treatment and text spacing. Self-hosted Inter substitutes for the unavailable licensed heading font. Both routes share LegalPage, SiteFooter and the existing navigation/scroll behavior. Footer Terms/Privacy and login utility links now work. Help and Discord use the owner's supplied invite, https://discord.gg/B5A5JX79k, in a separate tab.

The full third-party legal text was not reproduced. The pages contain independently written Rawan preview drafts based on the current website behavior. They do not adopt another company's prices, processors, deadlines, jurisdiction or contacts. The revision row identifies them as drafts, and search indexing is disabled until final policies are supplied. Copy, length and section count therefore differ from the reference. No agreement-acceptance flow was added.
